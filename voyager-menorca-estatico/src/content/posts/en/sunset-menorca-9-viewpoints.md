Menorca has sunsets that stick in your memory. Beyond the famous beaches, there are discreet viewpoints, lighthouses and cliffs where the sky lights up with oranges and violets. I’m **Daniel Vaquera**, official local guide for Voyager Menorca, and here are my 9 favorite places —with practical tips, recommended wind and little tricks— so you can enjoy the sunset like a Menorcan.

## When and how to best enjoy it

- **Arrive 40–50 minutes early.** The good colors start before the sun touches the horizon and explode 10–15 minutes after sunset.
- **Watch the wind.** If **tramontana (N)** is blowing, the north is clearer; with **migjorn (S)**, the south coast feels friendlier.
- **Safety and respect.** Closed shoes, flashlight for the return, and always away from the edge of the cliff. Leave no trace: Menorca is a [Biosphere Reserve](/en/menorca-biosphere-reserve/).

## 1) Pont d’en Gil (Ciutadella)

- **Why go:** a perfect natural arch to see how the sun “hides” behind the rock.
- **Access:** park near Camí de sa Farola and follow the path (10–15 min).
- **Local tip:** stay 10 minutes after sunset; the sky turns magenta and the arch silhouettes the scene like a painting.

## 2) Punta Nati Lighthouse (Ciutadella)

- **Why go:** lunar landscape, silence and a clear horizon to the northwest. The lighthouse was built in 1913 after the shipwreck of the *Général Chanzy*; you can find the full story in the [lighthouse guide](/en/guia-faros-menorca/).
- **Access:** narrow road; arrive early to park in the designated area.
- **Local tip:** take a jacket even in summer; the wind here is deceiving.

## 3) Cala Morell Viewpoints (Ciutadella)

- **Why go:** dramatic cliffs and white houses that are tinged with gold.
- **Access:** several natural terraces signposted above the development.
- **Local tip:** lean out (with caution) to the north end; there are usually fewer people and the angle is beautiful.

## 4) Cala en Brut (Ciutadella)

- **Why go:** rock platforms over an intense blue fjord.
- **Access:** easy from the development; ideal if you want to take a last evening swim.
- **Local tip:** find a low platform for reflections of “liquid gold” in the water.

## 5) Artrutx Lighthouse (Cap d’Artrutx – Ciutadella)

- **Why go:** iconic lighthouse with the sun setting to the southwest and views of Mallorca on clear days.
- **Access:** fully paved road; nearby cafeteria.
- **Local tip:** ideal for families or if you want a “comfortable” sunset with a chair and a drink. This is the option we recommend in [Menorca with children](/en/menorca-con-ninos-en-un-dia-ciutadella/).

## 6) Cavalleria Lighthouse (Es Mercadal)

- **Why go:** the highest cliff in the north and a 180° panoramic view.
- **Access:** road to the lighthouse; respect designated parking areas.
- **Local tip:** explore (staying on the paths) towards the west side to find quieter spots. Combine it with a prior swim at [Cavalleria beach](/en/las-mejores-playas-de-menorca-y-cuales-evitar-en-temporada-alta/).

## 7) Port de Sanitja (Es Mercadal)

- **Why go:** an ancient natural harbor —with a Roman past— and mirror-like sunsets.
- **Access:** next to Cavalleria; easy paths.
- **Local tip:** bring a tripod if you like photography: perfect reflections when the water is calm.

## 8) Cova d’en Xoroi (Cala en Porter – Alaior)

- **Why go:** the sun sinks in front of a cave hanging from the cliff: a very theatrical experience.
- **Access:** paid entrance and limited capacity; it’s advisable to arrive with plenty of time.
- **Local tip:** book the sunset pass and look for side balconies to avoid crowds.

## 9) Monte Toro (Es Mercadal)

- **Why go:** 358 m high —the island’s rooftop, with the sanctuary of the Virgen del Toro— to see how all of Menorca changes color, with 360° views.
- **Access:** road to the summit; parking next to the sanctuary.
- **Local tip:** if there’s airborne dust (calima), the sun turns intense red. Perfect for a telephoto lens.

## What to bring (quick list)

- **Water**, something to snack on and a **flashlight** for the return.
- **Light jacket**: even in August it cools down with the breeze.
- **Closed shoes** if you are going to cliffs.
- **Trash bag**: what you bring, you take with you.

## Bonus: sunrises in the east

If you’re an early riser, note **Favàritx**, **Sa Mesquida** or **Es Grau**: pastel skies and total silence. Sunrise in Menorca has a different kind of peace. In [September](/en/menorca-en-septiembre/), with the sun rising later, you don’t even have to get up so early.

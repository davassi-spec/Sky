Choosing the right beach can make the difference between a perfect day and one of traffic jams and full parking lots. Here is a clear and practical guide with recommendations, access information, and a foolproof trick to **avoid jellyfish**: follow the wind… but *in reverse*.

**Check the status of the parking lots before leaving:** 
[Menorca beach parking traffic light system (official link)](https://mou-tmenorca.com/ParkingsPlatges.aspx?IDIOMA=1&IDPUBDALT=9219).

## Recommended beaches

### 1) Cala Pregonda (North)

Golden-reddish sand, islets off the coast and very photogenic landscape. Wild environment without services.

- **Access:** from Binimel·là, ~25–30 min walk along the Camí de Cavalls.
- **Ideal for:** snorkeling and tranquility.
- **Tip:** bring *comfortable shoes* and enough *water/food*.

### 2) Platja de Cavalleria (North)

Large reddish sandy area, easy to combine with the lighthouse at sunset. Very limited services.

- **Access:** large parking lot and wooden walkways.
- **Tip:** respect the dunes and avoid using clay on your skin.

### 3) Cala Pilar (North)

Virgin spot between cliffs and pine forest. One of the most “wild” jewels.

- **Access:** ~35–45 min walk from the parking lot.
- **Tip:** with north wind (tramontana) there may be waves; choose another cove if you are looking for flat waters.

### 4) Son Bou (South)

The longest sandy beach in Menorca (about 2.5–3 km), with service areas and quieter areas.

- **Access:** simple, several accesses and parking.
- **Ideal for:** families and long walks.
- **Tip:** respect the dune system and use the signposted accesses.

### 5) Cala Binibeca / Binibèquer (Southeast)

Small and very transparent cove, near the famous white village.

- **Access:** comfortable, but it fills up quickly in summer.
- **Ideal for:** snorkeling and photography.
- **Tip:** go early or late to avoid crowds.

### 6) Cala Mitjana (South)

Turquoise waters among pine trees, 100% “Menorca” aesthetic. Mitjaneta is next door (very small).

- **Access:** easy path of ~15–20 min from the parking lot.
- **Services:** there are no beach bars; bring provisions.
- **Tip:** arrive early; the shade of the pine forest is appreciated on hot days.

## Beaches to *avoid* (in high season)

- **Cala Galdana:** very difficult to park; it is usually very crowded.
- **Macarella:** access by bus during high season.
- **Son Saura** and **Cala’n Turqueta:** parking lots fill up before 8:00 AM in summer.

**Smart Plan B:** if there is red on the traffic light, change zones without hesitation; Menorca has equally beautiful and more fluid alternatives.

## Anti-jellyfish trick: choose the coast *opposite* the wind

Jellyfish tend to accumulate where the wind pushes. **Golden rule:** if it blows from the north, go to the **south**; if it blows from the south, go to the **north**… and so on with everyone.

| Dominant wind | Best area | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **North** (Tramontana) | **South** | Son Bou, Binibeca, Mitjana |
| **South** (Migjorn) | **North** | Pregonda, Cavalleria, Pilar |
| **East** (Llevant/Xaloc) | **West–Northwest** | Cavalleria, Pregonda |
| **West** (Ponent/Llebeig) | **East–Southeast** | Binibeca |

*Tip:* check the wind forecast in the morning and decide accordingly.

## What to bring, no matter what

- **Water and food:** there are no beach bars in many coves.
- **Sun protection:** high SPF cream, cap/hat and sunglasses.
- **Comfortable shoes:** dirt/stone paths and walkways.
- **Waste bag:** virgin beaches = zero trash cans.
- **Towel or sarong** and, if you snorkel, **goggles/tube**.

## Quick logistics

- **Get up early or go late:** you will avoid heat and crowds.
- **Check the parking status:** 
[Parking traffic light system (official link)](https://mou-tmenorca.com/ParkingsPlatges.aspx?IDIOMA=1&IDPUBDALT=9219).
- **Respect the environment:** do not leave waste or step on the dunes; Menorca is a Biosphere Reserve.

## Summary

**Choose the beach according to the wind**, avoid the ultra-famous coves at peak hours, bring **provisions** and check the **parking status** before leaving. With these rules, Menorca always rewards.

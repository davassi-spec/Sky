**Menorca is not just sun and beach.** Behind its picture-perfect coves lie soulful experiences, tailor-made by those who know the island from the inside. I am **Daniel Vaquera**, official local guide at Voyager Menorca, and I want to show you those corners and experiences that make Menorca unforgettable. Here are **seven plans with history, flavor and landscape**.

## 1. Watch the Sunrise in Son Bou (out of Season)

Son Bou, with its miles of sand—the longest beach on the island—reveals its magic at dawn. Arriving before the day wakes up and watching the light color the sea is a silent gift that connects with the heart of the island. You can find more low-season ideas in our guide to [Menorca in September](/en/menorca-en-septiembre/).

## 2. Visit a Farm and Make your Own Mahón-Menorca Cheese

On a traditional Menorcan farm, you will feel the rhythm of the countryside as you participate in the production of cheese with designation of origin. **From milking to pressing it yourself**, it will be a culinary and emotional experience that you will take with you… literally.

## 3. Tour the Fish Market and Listen to the Fishermen

In Mahón or Ciutadella, the markets are a reflection of the sea. **You will learn to recognize** scorpion fish, red scorpion fish, or grouper while the fishermen tell stories that only the Mediterranean knows: tides, nets, and night shifts. And if you get hungry afterwards, you’ll find the best tables in our [2026 restaurant guide](/en/restaurantes-menorca-2026/).

## 4. Ascent to Monte Toro and its Meaning for the Menorcans

At 358 meters, Monte Toro is a **spiritual watchtower**: the highest point on the island and home to the sanctuary of the Virgen del Toro, the patron saint of Menorca. The views span the entire island and, on clear days, even Mallorca. It is a place where landscape and feeling meet.

## 5. Talayotic Site at Sunset

Among stones that hold more than 3,000 years of history, the sunset brings an ancestral peace. **Torralba d’en Salort or Torre d’en Galmés** turn golden while you listen to the echo of the past. If you’re curious, continue with our [guide to Talayotic Menorca](/en/menorca-talayotica-guia/), a World Heritage site since 2023.

## 6. Less Traveled Section of the Camí De Cavalls

This ancestral path that circles Menorca offers **more authentic corners** in its lesser-known sections. Unfiltered nature and landscapes that reconnect with the essence of the island. In the [Camí de Cavalls guide](/en/tourist-guide-cami-de-cavalls/), we tell you which section to choose depending on the day’s wind.

## 7. Gin Amb Llimonada in the Port of Ciutadella

To end the day, there is nothing like this local version of a gin and tonic with lemon—the traditional *pomada*, a legacy of the gin left by the British. **In one of those bars at the port**, with the sun setting and the boats swaying, every sip tastes like Menorca. If you want to explore the old town first, here is the [complete guide to Ciutadella](/en/what-to-see-in-ciutadella-menorca/).

**Menorca is a feeling.** It is waking up early, putting your feet in the cold sand, smelling the countryside while listening to the sounds of the sea and the sky. **If you want to discover it like this, step by step and hand in hand with someone who walks it every day… here I am.**

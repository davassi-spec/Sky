## What is a Biosphere Reserve?

UNESCO Biosphere Reserves are learning sites for sustainable development: territories where human life is reconciled with the conservation of nature and culture. More info on the official website of [UNESCO · MAB Programme](https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/wnbr/about).

![Footbridge over Mediterranean wetland for environmental education in Menorca](/images/pexels-photo-30269260.jpeg)

## Why Menorca is one (since 1993)

Menorca was declared a **Biosphere Reserve on October 7, 1993**. See the official file at [UNESCO · Menorca](https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/menorca) and the portal of the [Consell Insular de Menorca](https://www.menorcabiosfera.org/). In **2019**, the scope was extended to the entire marine environment (up to 12 nautical miles), reaching **514,485 ha**. Informative summary: [Menorca Preservation](https://menorcapreservation.org/protect-our-marine-environments/).

![Menorca coast with cliffs and turquoise sea, Biosphere Reserve](/images/pexels-photo-27404114.jpeg)

## Zoning: core, buffer, and transition

Management is organized into three zones: **core** (strict protection), **buffer** (compatible activities), and **transition** (daily life and sustainable economy). Full definition at [UNESCO · What are the Reserves?](https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/wnbr/about).

## Albufera des Grau: heart of the wetland

The **Parc Natural de s’Albufera des Grau** includes a lagoon, dunes, wild olive forests, coastline, and islets. It is the largest wetland area in Menorca and the *core zone* of the Reserve. Official info: [Govern Illes Balears · Parc Natural](https://www.caib.es/sites/espaisnaturalsprotegits/es/parc_natural_de_salbufera_des_grau/). The **Centre Rodríguez Femenías** offers maps, binoculars to borrow, and suggestions: [Illes Balears · Albufera des Grau](https://www.illesbalears.travel/en/menorca/nature-park-albufera-des-grau).

**Signposted itineraries** (easy level): [Mirador de Cala Llimpa, Santa Madrona and Sa Gola](https://www.caib.es/sites/espaisnaturalsprotegits/es/parc_natural_de_salbufera_des_grau/). PDF map: [Itineraries · PDF](https://www.caib.es/sites/espaisnaturalsprotegits/f/487585).

The park is part of **Natura 2000** (SCI/SPA **ES0000234**): file at [MITECO](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/biodiversidad/temas/espacios-protegidos/red-natura-2000/lic_baleares.html) and informative brochure [Natura 2000 East Coast (PDF)](https://www.miteco.gob.es/content/dam/miteco/es/biodiversidad/temas/conservacion-de-la-biodiversidad/folletoespaciosnatura2000_costaestemenorca_castellano_tcm30-542231.pdf).

## How to visit responsibly (10 keys)

1. **Stay on authorized trails** (Cala Llimpa, Santa Madrona and Sa Gola).
2. **Observe wildlife silently** and from a distance; do not approach nests or hiding places.
3. **Zero waste:** everything that comes in, goes out with you; avoid single-use plastics.
4. **Respect posidonia and dunes:** do not step on or uproot vegetation; do not move driftwood.
5. **Water and sun:** bring a water bottle and protection; avoid peak sun hours in summer.
6. **No collecting of species** (plants, shells, fossils).
7. **Dogs** always on a leash and away from sensitive bird areas.
8. **No drones** in sensitive areas or without specific permits.
9. **Local economy:** prioritize crafts, km0 products, and responsible services.
10. **Responsible transport:** share a car, use bus or bike when possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the best time to go?

All year round. In autumn and spring there is a greater passage of birds; in summer we recommend the first hours of the day.

### Is it suitable for families?

Yes, the official itineraries are easy and well signposted.

### Is it possible to get there without a car?

In season there are bus/taxi combinations to Es Grau. If you need it, we coordinate the logistics.

### Can I use a drone?

Not in sensitive wildlife areas or without permits. Ask us first to avoid penalties.

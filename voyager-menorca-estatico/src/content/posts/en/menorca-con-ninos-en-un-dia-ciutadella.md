## Why this route works with kids

The secret is to combine **short stretches**, places with **shade and restrooms**, and a long time for **playing on the beach**. From Ciutadella, everything is close by, and we reduce travel.

- **Flexible pace:** stops every 20–30 minutes.
- **Learning + play:** short stories and visual challenges (shields, portals, boats).
- **Living plan:** we choose the beach according to the *wind of the day* to find a better sea.

**Duration**1 full day
**Ages**3–12 (adaptable)
**Stroller**Yes (morning and lighthouse)
**Best time**Spring–autumn

## Morning (9:30–11:30 AM): Ciutadella made easy

We start when the sun is still gentle. Flat walk through the historic center, with shade and corners that kids love.

### Walking route (90–120 min)

- **Plaça des Born**: obelisk and views of the old port.
- **Cathedral**: facade and cloister (when open).
- **Streets with palaces**: giant doorways and shields.
- **Mercat del Peix / surroundings**: color and local products.
- **Descent to the port**: boats, shipyards, and photos.

Mini-game: How many *lions* do you see on the shields? Which door is the largest?

### Quick tips

- Hat and water from the start.
- Restrooms available in bars/market (consumption recommended).
- Stroller: smooth sidewalks, some streets with cobblestones.

## Midday (12:00–3:30 PM): Beach depending on the wind

We choose a sheltered beach depending on whether *Tramontana*, *Migjorn*, or *Llevant* is blowing. This usually means better sea for the children.

### What wind is blowing today?

- **Tramontana (N):** better to the *south* (e.g., Son Saura, Cala Galdana).
- **Migjorn (S/SW):** better to the *north* (e.g., Algaiarens/La Vall).
- **Llevant (E):** shelter to the *west* (e.g., Cala Blanca, Cala en Blanes).

More useful info: [Beaches of Menorca depending on the wind](/en/las-mejores-playas-de-menorca-y-cuales-evitar-en-temporada-alta/).

### Eat & rest

- Simple picnic and plenty of cold water.
- Umbrella/parasol or pine area.
- Flip-flops + jelly shoes for rocks.
- Mineral cream for sensitive skin.

## Nap and ice cream (4:00–5:00 PM)

Return to Ciutadella for a quiet break. Artisan ice cream, playground, and, if it’s hot, a little shade.

## Afternoon (5:30–8:00 PM): two options

### A) Pont d’en Gil and west coast

Easy and flat walk to the natural arch. Ideal for sunset photos. Closed footwear recommended.

### B) Artrutx Lighthouse

Easy access and simple parking. Sunset with views of Mallorca on clear days.

### Guide tip

With *strong Tramontana*, the lighthouse is usually more comfortable than the cliffs. With *Migjorn*, Pont d’en Gil is very pleasant.

## Plan B if it rains or is very windy

- Longer cultural visit in Ciutadella (cloisters, palaces, exhibition halls).
- Craft workshops and local tastings indoors.
- Lighthouse route by car with short stops. More in [our lighthouse article](/en/guia-faros-menorca/).

## Family checklist

- Children’s hats + sunglasses
- 2 bottles of frozen water
- Savory snacks and fruit
- Umbrella and large towel
- UV shirt and jelly shoes
- Mineral sunscreen
- Small first aid kit (band-aids, saline solution)
- Waste bag

## Logistics and parking

- **Historic center:** signposted public parking and blue zone (check schedules).
- **Beaches:** arrive early in summer; respect signs and access points.
- **Sunset:** calculate 30–40 min extra for traffic in high season.

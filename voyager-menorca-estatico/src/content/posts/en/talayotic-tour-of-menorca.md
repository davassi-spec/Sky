## What is Talayotic Menorca (UNESCO)?

**Talayotic Menorca** was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 2023 for its cyclopean architecture and its exceptional testimony to prehistoric life in the Mediterranean. The site encompasses dozens of sites integrated into traditional agricultural landscapes. If you want the full context—chronology, monument types, and how to visit respectfully—check out our [complete guide to Talayotic Menorca](/en/menorca-talayotica-guia/).

## Key tour sites

### Naveta des Tudons (Ciutadella)

Funerary monument with a horseshoe plan and two superimposed chambers, unique in the world. It is Menorca’s archaeological icon and a quick and photogenic stop.

- **Visit**: 20–30 min
- **Access**: short and signposted path from the parking lot
- **Tip**: first or last hour for soft light and fewer people

### Torralba d’en Salort (Alaior)

Complex with **talayots**, hypostyle hall and one of the most impressive **taulas** on the island. In season, it organizes special activities at sunset or at night.

- **Visit**: 45–60 min
- **Best**: the monumental taula and the open landscape
- **Extra**: *Sunset* / *Starlight* program depending on dates

### Torre d’en Galmés (Alaior)

The largest Talayotic settlement in Menorca, on a strategic hill with views and an interpretation center. It features water collection systems and circular dwellings.

- **Visit**: 60–90 min
- **Services**: picnic area, accessible sections
- **Tip**: hat and water in summer; ideal with a light breeze

## Practical information

### Best time

All year round. In summer, first/last hours; in winter, avoid days of extreme wind and mud after rains.

### What to bring

- Closed shoes with gripping soles
- Water (1–1.5 L per person) and some food
- Sun protection or windbreaker depending on the season

### Accessibility

Uneven terrain overall. *Torre d’en Galmés* offers accessible sections and picnic tables. We adapt the pace and stops for your comfort.

### Rules

- Do not climb on walls or touch structures
- Drones/tripods: only with permission
- Respect signage and private properties

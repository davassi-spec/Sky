## Ciutadella

### Café Balear · €€€ *(seafood · stew)*

I ordered the **lobster stew**: deep broth, toasted bread for dipping, and that embracing harbor aroma. A classic that never disappoints. 
📍 [Passeig des Moll, Ciutadella](https://maps.google.com/?q=Caf%C3%A9+Balear+Ciutadella)

### S’Amarador · €€ *(rice dishes · harbor)*

The **creamy lobster rice** was perfectly cooked. Flavorful, clean, and ideal for sharing while watching the boats. 
📍 [Port Antic, Ciutadella](https://maps.google.com/?q=S%27Amarador+Ciutadella)

### Es Tast de na Sílvia · €€ *(km0 · slow food)*

The **Mahón cheese tasting** (mild, semi-cured, and cured) tasted like pure Menorcan landscape: milk, salt, and patience. 
📍 [C. Santa Clara, Ciutadella](https://maps.google.com/?q=Es+Tast+de+na+S%C3%ADlvia+Ciutadella)

### Rels · €€€ *(signature · traditional)*

I loved their **reinterpreted oliaigua**: tradition and technique in a fresh dish, with crispy “codina” as a local nod. 
📍 [C. Sant Isidre, Ciutadella](https://maps.google.com/?q=Rels+Ciutadella)

### Ulisses (Mercat) · €€ *(tapas · market)*

At the bar, I had **grilled red prawns** and **lobster stew croquettes**. Two maritime bites worth repeating. 
📍 [Plaça de la Llibertat, Ciutadella](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ulisses+Mercat+Ciutadella)

## Maó / Mahón

### El Rais · €€€ *(rice dishes · harbor)*

The **creamy cuttlefish and prawn rice** won me over with its balance and depth. Unpretentious seafood cuisine facing the harbor. 
📍 [Moll de Llevant, Maó](https://maps.google.com/?q=El+Rais+Mah%C3%B3n)

### Ses Forquilles · €€ *(tapas · creative)*

Their **roasted aubergines with honey** have an addictive citrusy touch. Perfect for a long and enjoyable tapas session. 
📍 [Centro histórico, Maó](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ses+Forquilles+Mah%C3%B3n)

### El Romero · €€€ *(sustainable · sea)*

The **grilled fish of the day** arrived with vegetables and was perfectly cooked. Quality produce and respect for ingredients come first. 
📍 [Moll de Llevant, Maó](https://maps.google.com/?q=El+Romero+Mah%C3%B3n)

### Can Vermut · € *(vermouth · Mahón cheese)*

A happy stop for a **Mahón-Menorca cheese board** with pickles and a chilled vermouth. A perfect midday plan. 
📍 [C. d’Es Moro, Maó](https://maps.google.com/?q=Can+Vermut+Mah%C3%B3n)

### Cap Roig (Sa Mesquida) · €€€ *(viewpoint · stew)*

With open sea views, their **lobster stew** tastes of the Tramontana wind. Landscape and spoon go hand in hand. 
📍 [Sa Mesquida, Maó](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cap+Roig+Sa+Mesquida)

## Es Castell

### Sa Punta · €€€ *(views · fish)*

The **salt-baked fish** was juicy and clean, with olive oil elevating it even further. An unforgettable sunset in Cales Fonts. 
📍 [Cales Fonts, Es Castell](https://maps.google.com/?q=Sa+Punta+Es+Castell)

### Trébol · €€ *(seafood · classic)*

A very tasty **arroz a banda**, with mild aioli and postcard views of the harbor. 
📍 [Moll de Cales Fonts, Es Castell](https://maps.google.com/?q=Restaurante+Trebol+Es+Castell)

### Tarambana · €€ *(terrace · Mediterranean)*

Their **tuna tartare** with citrus notes won me over. Fresh, balanced, and with views to linger over. 
📍 [Moll de Cales Fonts, Es Castell](https://maps.google.com/?q=Tarambana+Es+Castell)

### Chèspir (Cales Fonts) · €€ *(tapas · harbor)*

**Patatas bravas with mild aioli** and tapas with a maritime feel. Intimate atmosphere right on the waterfront. 
📍 [Moll de Cales Fonts, 47, Es Castell](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ch%C3%A8spir+Cales+Fonts)

### Nikki’s · €€ *(meats)*

The **grilled sirloin steak** was perfectly cooked and came with a generous side dish. Attentive service and a relaxed pace. 
📍 [Moll de Cales Fonts, Es Castell](https://maps.google.com/?q=Nikki%27s+Es+Castell)

## Es Mercadal (includes Fornells)

### Es Cranc (Fornells) · €€€ *(lobster stew · iconic)*

The benchmark **lobster stew**: taste of the rocks, powerful broth, and tradition on the table. 
📍 [Fornells, Es Mercadal](https://maps.google.com/?q=Es+Cranc+Fornells)

### Sa Llagosta (Fornells) · €€€ *(lobster · beachfront)*

I’ll go with the **lobster carpaccio**: delicate, citrusy, and absolutely Menorcan. 
📍 [C. Gabriel Gelabert, 12, Fornells](https://maps.google.com/?q=Sa+Llagosta+Fornells)

### Sa Fonda (Port de Fornells) · €€ *(seafood)*

A flavorful and well-bound **arroz del “señoret”**. Honest cuisine overlooking the pier. 
📍 [Passeig Marítim, Port de Fornells](https://maps.google.com/?q=Sa+Fonda+Fornells)

### Ca Na Marga (Es Mercadal) · €€ *(grill · meat)*

The **grilled beef** arrived with just the right smokiness and supreme juiciness. Ideal for meat lovers. 
📍 [Ctra. Fornells, Es Mercadal](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ca+Na+Marga+Es+Mercadal)

### Molí d’es Racó (Es Mercadal) · €€ *(traditional · mill)*

Their **kid goat in sauce** tastes of slow-cooked, traditional cuisine. An iconic spot in an old mill. 
📍 [C. Major / Verge del Toro, Es Mercadal](https://maps.google.com/?q=Mol%C3%AD+d%27es+Rac%C3%B3+Es+Mercadal)

## Sant Lluís

### Bodegas Binifadet · €€€ *(winery · terrace)*

I toasted with a local white wine and an impeccable **Mahón cheese with olive oil**. Garden, grapes, and calm. 
📍 [Camí de Ses Barraques, Sant Lluís](https://maps.google.com/?q=Binifadet+Sant+Llu%C3%ADs)

### Sa Pedrera d’es Pujol · €€€ *(signature)*

The **roasted meat ravioli with truffle** were pure elegance. Technique serving flavor.

### Salitre (Cala Torret) · €€€ *(sea · views)*

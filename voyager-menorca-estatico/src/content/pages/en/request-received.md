Thank you for trusting us. We have received your request and are already reviewing it.

What happens now

We check availability

We review the date you have chosen and the guide’s schedule. We always do this in **less than 24 hours**.

We send you an email with the payment link

If there’s availability, you will receive an email with the booking summary and a **secure link to complete payment**. Until you pay, there is no commitment.

Confirmation and meeting point

Once payment is confirmed, we will send you the confirmation with the exact meeting point, time, and the guide’s phone number.

**Check your spam folder.** If you haven’t received anything within 24 hours, our email has probably ended up there. Write to us and we’ll resolve it immediately.

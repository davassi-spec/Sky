## Discover Ciutadella and Maó: the Urban Jewels of Menorca

**Ciutadella**, the former capital of Menorca, is a city full of charm. Its cobbled streets, historic squares, and noble buildings such as the Episcopal Palace and the Cathedral of Menorca bear witness to its rich past. During the tour, you will learn about the legends and anecdotes hidden in its corners, enjoying the authenticity of its old town and the bustle of the port.

On the other hand, **Maó** is a city with a very different but equally captivating essence. Its imposing natural harbor, one of the largest in the Mediterranean, contrasts with the urban network of colonial houses and historic buildings such as the Church of Santa María and the Museum of Menorca. You will immerse yourself in the local culture, discovering markets, traditional cafes, and emblematic places that reflect the cosmopolitan character of the city.

## Heritage, Culture and Traditions: the Essence of Menorca

Menorca is much more than beaches and nature; its **history and traditions** are an essential part of its identity. Through our cultural itineraries, you will discover **Talayotic Menorca**, with its imposing prehistoric monuments, authentic vestiges of an ancient civilization. We will visit emblematic sites such as **the taulas, the talayots and the navetas**, while learning about their meaning and their connection with island life.

In addition, we will explore the most traditional side of Menorca, touring rural villages where artisanal trades are still practiced as they were centuries ago. You will learn about the essence of its customs, from the elaboration of local products to typical festivities that reflect the Menorcan soul. Immerse yourself in a cultural journey that connects the past with the present, always guided by the passion and knowledge of Daniel Vaquera.

## Biosphere Reserve and Starlight: Nature and Starry Skies of Menorca

Menorca, declared a **Biosphere Reserve by UNESCO**, is a natural paradise where biodiversity coexists with unique landscapes. We will take you to explore its protected natural spaces, such as wetlands, ravines, and coastal areas, where native fauna and flora flourish in their pure state. Enjoy guided routes that connect nature and sustainability in unique environments.

In addition, Menorca has one of the cleanest skies in Europe, making it an ideal destination for astronomical observation. Immerse yourself in the Starlight experience, where you will discover constellations, planets, and stars under an impressive celestial dome. A perfect combination of nature and astronomy to enjoy both day and night.

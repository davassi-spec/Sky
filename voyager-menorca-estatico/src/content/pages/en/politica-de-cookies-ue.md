This policy explains which storage technologies **voyagermenorca.com** uses, what they are for and how you can control them. Controller: Daniel Vaquera Sintes (info@voyagermenorca.com).

## What we use

| Technology | Purpose | When it is active | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cookie preference (`vm_consent_v1`, local storage) | Remember your cookie choice | Always (technical, exempt from consent) | Until you clear it |
| Google Analytics 4 | Anonymous usage statistics to improve the site | **Only if you click “Accept”** | Up to 14 months |
| Bookings (Turitop) | Third-party online booking system | Only when you use the booking button | Per Turitop's policy |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Anti-spam protection for forms | Only if enabled, when a form loads | Session |

We do not use advertising or profiling cookies. Fonts are served from this site (no connection to Google Fonts).

## How to change your choice

Click “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page to accept or reject analytics cookies. You can also clear site data in your browser.

## An Accredited Official Guide at your Service

I’m Daniel Vaquera, and my purpose goes beyond showing you a beautiful place. I want you to feel the true essence of this island, to take a piece of Menorca with you in your heart.

As your **Accredited Official Guide**, my passion is to share with you the secrets that don’t appear on the maps: I’ll take you to those hidden coves with turquoise waters that we locals keep to ourselves, we’ll stroll through towns where history comes to life in every stone, and together we’ll seek out the most magical sunsets from the cliffs of the north to the port of Ciutadella.

Each experience is designed for you to immerse yourself in the authentic Menorca. Are you coming?

## My Commitment: to You and to Menorca

I have been dedicating myself to tourism for **more than a decade**, but my connection with Menorca comes from a lifetime. This deep love for my land is the engine of everything I do. On my routes, I don’t just share historical data; I weave for you the legends that I was told as a child and give you those personal recommendations that make the difference between a visit and an experience.

My love for Menorca goes hand in hand with my commitment to protect it. That’s why I firmly believe in sustainable tourism. Each experience I design is respectful of our incredible environment and seeks to support local artisans and producers.

Because for me, traveling is not just seeing, it’s **connecting**. It is learning, enjoying the present moment, and taking home much more than a photo: an unforgettable memory. I invite you to discover the island under this gaze.

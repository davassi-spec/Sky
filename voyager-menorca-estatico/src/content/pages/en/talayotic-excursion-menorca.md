## The three sites on the route

![Naveta des Tudons](/images/naveta20des20tudons20-20001.jpg)

**01 · CIUTADELLA**

### Naveta des Tudons

Menorca’s most iconic funerary monument. Its architecture and the findings from its interior allow us to discuss death rituals in the Bronze Age.

![Taula at Torralba d'en Salort](/images/taula-de-torralba-d-en-salort-menorca-illes-ballears-webp.webp)

**02 · ALAIOR**

### Torralba d’en Salort

One of the most spectacular taulas on the island. Here we interpret the ceremonial enclosure, the talayots, and the community life of the settlement.

![Interior courtyard of the Círculo Cartailhac at Torre d'en Galmés](/images/img-2393-editar-editar.jpg)

**03 · ALAIOR**

### Torre d’en Galmés

One of the most extensive Talayotic settlements in the Balearic Islands: houses, talayots, hypostyle rooms, and an ingenious water collection system.

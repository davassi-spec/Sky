Discover the secrets of Mahón, the vibrant capital of Menorca, marked by its impressive natural harbor—one of the largest in the world—and its fascinating British heritage. Accompanied by your **official local guide**, you will stroll through a network of streets that blend Georgian elegance with the Mediterranean soul.

**What will you discover on this tour?**

- The **Plaça de s’Esplanada**, the social heart of the city.
- The unique architecture of its **Georgian-style buildings**, a legacy of the 18th century.
- The **Teatro Principal de Mahón**, one of the oldest opera houses in Spain.
- The historic **Mercado del Claustro del Carmen**, full of life and local products.
- The Town Hall (Sa Sala) and the **Church of Santa María**, with its imposing facade.
- Strategic viewpoints with spectacular views of the port.

**Languages:** Spanish, English, and French. **Duration:** 2 hours.

**Includes:**

- Walking tour with an accredited official guide.
- Detailed historical and cultural explanations.

**Does not include:**

- Tickets to monuments that require a ticket (optional).
- Transportation to the meeting point.
- Tips.

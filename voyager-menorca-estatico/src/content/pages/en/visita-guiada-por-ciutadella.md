Let yourself be captivated by the aristocratic charm of Ciutadella, the island’s former capital. With the help of a **local official guide**, you’ll be immersed in a journey through time, wandering its cobbled streets, stately palaces, and corners full of legends. Feel the pulse of a city that breathes history in every stone.

**What will you discover on this tour?**

- The imposing **Plaça des Born**, the city’s nerve center and witness to its history.
- The exteriors of the **Town Hall**, the elegant **Teatre des Born**, and the palaces that surround it.
- The charm of the **natural harbor of Ciutadella**, with its traditional boats and seafaring atmosphere.
- The majestic naves of the **Cathedral-Basilica of Santa María de Menorca**, a jewel of Catalan Gothic architecture.
- The secrets of the **Monastery of Santa Clara** and the stories held within its walls.
- Anecdotes and local curiosities that only an island guide can tell you.

**Languages:** Spanish, English, and French. **Duration:** 2 hours.

**Includes:**

- Walking tour with an accredited official guide.
- Detailed historical and cultural explanations.

**Does not include:**

- Tickets to monuments that require a ticket (optional).
- Transportation to the meeting point.
- Tips.

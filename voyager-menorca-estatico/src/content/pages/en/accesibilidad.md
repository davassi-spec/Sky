Our commitment is to make this website accessible, in accordance with Law 11/2023 of May 8, transposing European Union Directives on accessibility for certain products and services, as well as with the applicable current regulations on accessibility and non-discrimination.

### SITUACIÓN DE CUMPLIMIENTO

This website is partially compliant with the applicable accessibility requirements due to the exceptions and the non-compliance of certain aspects indicated below.

### Non-accessible content

The content listed below may not be accessible for the following reasons: 
There may be some contrast issues that do not meet the minimum requirements in relation to adjacent background colors. 
There may be some non-text elements that are not correctly labeled. 
There may be some automatic behaviors in search or interaction components that do not always provide the level of control expected by the user. 
There may be some texts in other languages that are not correctly labeled. 
There may be occasional editing code errors on some pages. 
There may be office documents in PDF or other formats published before this statement was reviewed that do not fully meet all accessibility requirements, although efforts have been made to ensure that most of them do comply.

### PREPARATION OF THE FOLLOWING ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

The method used to prepare this statement was a self-assessment carried out by the person responsible for web development.

### COMMENTS AND CONTACT DETAILS

You can send communications regarding accessibility requirements, for example: 
• Report any possible non-compliance by this website. 
• Inform us of other difficulties accessing the content. 
• Submit any other query or suggestion for improvement related to the website’s accessibility.

You can also request information about specific content and report accessibility-related issues through the contact channels provided on this website. In your request, we recommend that you clearly indicate the page or content affected, the issue identified and, where applicable, the proposed improvement or the information you need to receive in an accessible format.

### REVIEW AND IMPROVEMENT PROCEDURE

We work continuously to improve the accessibility of this website and correct any issues that are identified. If you encounter any access barrier, you can let us know through the contact channels published on the website so we can review it and, where appropriate, take the necessary measures.

### OPTIONAL CONTENT

This portal is designed to allow you to adjust text size and other display options using the standard settings of browsers. 
If you want to change the text font size in the main graphical browsers, you can use the following options: 
• Chrome, Edge and Firefox: Ctrl + + to increase it, Ctrl + – to decrease it, and Ctrl + 0 to restore the original size. 
• Safari: Command + + to increase it, Command + – to decrease it, and Command + 0 to restore it. 
You can also change the size of all page content using the browser’s zoom options. 
If you want to further customize the visual presentation, such as colors or styles, you can use the accessibility options in your browser or operating system.

## The route, told with meaning

**01 · PLAÇA DE LA CONSTITUCIÓ**

### Maó’s historic heart

We start by the Town Hall and Santa Maria. Here we trace the city’s medieval origins, its growth, and the constant relationship between the centre and the harbour.

**02 · CARRERS I MIRADORS**

### The British legacy

Sash windows, colourful façades, words and customs recall the periods of British rule in the 18th century. We also stop at the Pont de Sant Roc, a remnant of the old city walls.

**03 · CLAUSTRE DEL CARME**

### Market, produce and local life

We go into the former Carme convent, now a market and meeting point. We talk about Menorcan cuisine, trade, and how the city is lived day to day.

**04 · PORT DE MAÓ**

### A harbour that changed history

From the viewpoints, we understand why one of the Mediterranean’s great natural harbours drew fleets, merchants and European powers. Its geography explains a large part of Menorca’s modern history.

## Santa Maria and its monumental organ

We go into the church to discover its architecture and one of the island’s great musical treasures: the organ built in the early 19th century, with four keyboards and more than 3,000 pipes. Admission is included.

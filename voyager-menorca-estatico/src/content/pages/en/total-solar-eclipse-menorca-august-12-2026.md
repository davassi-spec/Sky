## The Evening, Minute by Minute

**7:37 PM · Partial phase begins**

The Moon begins to cover the solar disk. We will use certified glasses throughout this phase.

**8:30:09 PM · Totality arrives**

The diamond ring and solar corona appear. The light changes, and the lunar shadow sweeps across the landscape.

**8:31:21 PM · Light returns**

Totality ends; the Sun will set a few minutes later, around 8:42 PM.

## Each spot includes ISO 12312-2 glasses

Looking at the Sun without adequate protection can cause permanent damage. Regular sunglasses are not sufficient. The guide will explain when they should be used and the only moment they can be removed: during totality.

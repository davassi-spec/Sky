Data will not be transferred to third parties, except legal obligation or if it is necessary for the provision of the requested service. In such cases, the data may be shared with:

International data transfers outside the European Economic Area (EEA) will not be carried out without the prior consent of the user.

## User Rights

The user has the right to:

- Request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Request the deletion of their data when they are no longer necessary.
- Oppose the processing of their data for specific purposes.

- Request the portability of their data to another controller.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, the user can send an email to info@voyagermenorca.com.

## Double Kayak Excursion Caves Route - Cala'n Porter

An outing as a couple or family along the coast with the most sea caves on the island.

**Operator:** Excursion carried out by a collaborating partner of Voyager Menorca.

We will paddle along limestone cliffs and enter—when the sea allows—cavities of turquoise waters. An easy, photogenic experience, perfect for those who want to get started in kayaking.

### What you will see

- Stretches of unspoiled coastline with high cliffs and sheltered coves.
- Sea caves and natural arches; depending on conditions, a short stop for snorkeling in a quiet cove (Cales Coves area).
- **“Double” format**: ideal for 2 + 1.
- **Self-bailing double kayak** (very stable and comfortable).
- Possibility of a third child seat (small children as 3rd occupant; subject to availability).

### Duration and schedules

- **Duration:** 3 hours of activity.
- **Departures:** morning and afternoon; we assign the best time according to wind and availability (usual references: 09:00, 10:15, 12:00, 16:00, 16:30).

### Meeting point

Cala en Porter

Es Canutells

### Includes

- Self-bailing double kayak, vest, paddles, and waterproof container.
- Guide throughout the route.
- Complimentary photo report with action camera.

### What to bring

- Swimsuit and sun shirt, hat, and sunscreen.
- Water and a snack, towel, and water shoes.
- Snorkel mask (recommended). Secure prescription/sunglasses if you use them.

### Level and requirements

- **Difficulty:** low (technical) | **Effort:** medium.
- **Essential** to know how to swim.
- **Age:** minors accompanied by an adult; from 14 years old they can paddle in their seat.
- **Main language of the guide:** Spanish (support in English depending on the instructor).

### Environmental policy

Red Natura 2000

### Important notices

- Activity subject to sea and wind conditions. The itinerary may vary for safety.
- Limited places. Material and child seats subject to availability.
- The operation, insurance, and billing correspond to the collaborating partner.

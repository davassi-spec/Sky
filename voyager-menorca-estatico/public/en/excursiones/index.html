Before booking

What you should know

If your situation is special, write to me. It is easier to design a good visit when I know the group.

How do I book an excursion?

Enter the experience and send the request. Daniel will personally confirm availability and details.

Is the price per person or per group?

Private tours have a flat rate per group.

Can the route be adapted?

Yes. The pace and content are adjusted to interests, ages, and mobility needs.

What happens if I have to cancel?

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. No advance payment is required for private routes.

Menorca with context

An island better understood with company

Behind every square, palace, and prehistoric monument are decisions, conflicts, and ways of life that do not appear in brochures. A local official guide connects each place with the island’s history.

Voyager Menorca works with private groups and is accredited with the Menorca Biosphere Reserve brand.
